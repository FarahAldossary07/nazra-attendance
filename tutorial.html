How Nazra works

Nazra recognizes each employee by the area around their eyes, so it works even while the niqab is worn. Everything runs privately inside this browser — the camera video never leaves your device.

Part 1 · Enroll an employee (do this once per person)

  1. Open the Enroll employee page and allow camera access when the browser asks.
  2. Type the person's full name and employee ID.
  3. Look at the camera the way you'll appear day-to-day — niqab on. There's no need to uncover; Nazra only ever uses the area around your eyes.
  4. Click ⚡ Capture 20, slowly turning your head a little and leaning in/out so the samples cover natural variation. Let the niqab sit slightly differently a couple of times too.
  5. Aim for about 40 samples, then click 💾 Save employee. Repeat for each person.

Part 2 · Clock in / out at the kiosk

  1. Open the kiosk page. Stand so your eyes are clearly in view.
  2. The box shows UNKNOWN, then flips to Employee ID, Name once you're recognized.
  3. A banner says “BLINK TO LOG ATTENDANCE.” Blink — this proves you're a live person, not a photo.
  4. You'll see “LOG SUCCESSFUL” with the time, and the entry appears in Today's log. Nazra automatically alternates IN / OUT for each person per day.
  5. Use ⬇︎ Export CSV to download the full attendance history.

Tips for good accuracy

  • Enroll in the same room and lighting where the kiosk will be used.
  • Even overhead light is best; avoid strong backlight or glare on glasses.
  • If someone reads UNKNOWN too often, enroll a few more samples for them.
  • The match number on the kiosk shows confidence — higher is a stronger match.

Good to know

  • Data stays on this device. Enrollments and the log live in this browser only. Enroll on the machine you'll use as the kiosk.
  • Clearing the browser's site data will erase enrollments — export your CSV to keep records.
  • This is a concept demo; for a shared, multi-device system the data would move to a central database.
